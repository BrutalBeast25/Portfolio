01. About Me

Who I Am

I'm a Data Engineer & Analyst based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, with 3 years of hands-on experience in the renewable energy industry. I work at the intersection of operational technology and data — pulling raw telemetry from wind turbines, solar arrays, SCADA systems and IoT devices and turning it into actionable insights.

My pipelines run on Python, push data to AWS S3, power real-time dashboards in Power BI and ThingsBoard, and fire instant Telegram alerts when turbines fault. I also led a full production platform migration with just two hours of downtime.

Currently extending this into AI — building a natural-language assistant that lets operators ask plain-English questions and get turbine data back instantly, without writing a single line of SQL.
